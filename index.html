About

Looking for a clean look? Time to get Filthy…

All pieces are handmade with stainless steel rings, link by link, that will withstand the wear and tear you might experience. Stainless steel jewelry comes in a variety of finishes—matte, brushed, glossy, and more—allowing for versatile styling that complements both casual and formal attire.

Stainless steel jewelry is highly valued for its durability and low maintenance—resisting rust, tarnish, and scratches—keeping its appearance with minimal care. Its hypoallergenic, biocompatible properties make it a suitable option for individuals with sensitive skin or metal allergies, making it a practical and safe choice for everyday wear.

For us, the environment is everything, and recyclability is important. Stainless steel is infinitely recyclable with a high recovery rate and produces less waste during production.

Charms and pendants may be made from other metals, as some are sourced from vintage sources and other distributors.
